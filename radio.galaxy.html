Radio.Galaxy - An AR-based VLBI Game

In collaboration with colleagues at the University of Würzburg and the University of Applied Sciences Hof, we have developed an AR based game to make the VLBI technique accessible to the broad public. The App is currently in the final stages of development and can be obtained by contacting me. Additional material like the map and telescope markers can be downloaded on this page. If you want to attach the telescope markers to physical telescope miniatures, I recommend 3D-printing the 3D-ALMA model by ESO.
